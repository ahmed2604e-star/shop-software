Sale ka tareeqa

Sale save hote hi stock se quantity minus hogi. Munafa = sale amount − sold items ki purchase cost. Dashboard mein aaj ki sale aur andazati munafa nazar aayega.

Agar customer ko udhaar diya hai, to is demo mein sale phir bhi sale amount mein count hoti hai. Udhaar/received payment ka separate ledger is version mein shamil nahi.
